DETAILED COURSE CURRICULUM

14 weeks. From capture to client delivery.

12 weeks of core technical training + 2 free weeks of business, marketing & AI growth.

WEEK 01Professional Wedding Shooting & Camera Mastery+

Camera handling, lens choices and lighting for wedding production.

  • Sony/Canon camera handling, lens selection, shutter angles and Log/Cine picture profiles.
  • Three-point lighting, gimbal balancing, bride-and-groom posing and wedding shoot simulation.
WEEK 02Wedding Photo Editing Workflow+

Build a consistent photo editing workflow in Photoshop and Lightroom.

  • Photoshop CC, Lightroom Classic and Camera Raw workspace fundamentals.
  • RAW processing, exposure recovery, white balance, colour correction and skin-tone adjustments.
WEEK 03Advanced Photo Retouching & Manipulation+

Refine portraits while preserving natural detail.

  • Micro and macro frequency separation; non-destructive dodge and burn.
  • Eye and hair enhancement, skin texture preservation, backdrop work and composite creation.
WEEK 04AI & High-Speed Bulk Photo Editing+

Develop a repeatable workflow for larger wedding photo sets.

  • Batch processing, preset synchronisation and consistent event-wide adjustments.
  • AI filters, skin softening, Generative Fill background work and AI upscaling.
WEEK 05Wedding Album Design & Storyboarding+

Shape individual photographs into a wedding story.

  • Page grids, margins, bleed settings and album design setup.
  • Haldi-to-reception sequencing, template customisation and cinematic typography.
WEEK 06Album Finishes, Printing & Client Delivery+

Prepare polished albums for review, printing and delivery.

  • Velvet, leather and acrylic cover options; lab colour-profile preparation.
  • Proofing, digital approval, packaging and client presentation.
WEEK 07Traditional Wedding Editing with EDIUS Pro+

Edit multi-camera wedding footage for clear, efficient delivery.

  • EDIUS interface, multi-track workflow and two-to-three-camera synchronisation.
  • Traditional titles, music placement, fast cutting and delivery exports.
WEEK 08Cinematic Editing with Premiere Pro+

Build an organised timeline and a coherent wedding story.

  • Project organisation, proxies, sequence settings, J-cuts and L-cuts.
  • Speed ramps, whip zooms, invisible cuts and dialogue/music balance.
WEEK 09Advanced Storytelling & Motion Graphics+

Use pacing and sound to strengthen the emotional flow.

  • Pacing, tension, sound effects and ambient audio layering.
  • Motion graphic titles, lower thirds and Premiere Pro sound tools.
WEEK 10Music Videos, Teasers & Reels+

Adapt the edit for short formats and online delivery.

  • Beat-marker synchronisation, wedding teasers and vertical 9:16 editing.
  • Retention-focused editing, thumbnails and 4K export settings.
WEEK 11DaVinci Resolve Colour Grading+

Control colour, contrast and skin tones with a structured grading workflow.

  • Nodes, lift/gamma/gain, HDR wheels, curves and qualifiers.
  • Power windows, skin-tone evaluation, LUT management and look recreation.
WEEK 12Fusion VFX, Audio & Final Wedding Film+

Bring your technical and storytelling skills into a finished project.

  • Fusion titles, object removal and sky replacement; Fairlight audio mastering.
  • Final wedding film submission, critique and portfolio showreel finalisation.
WEEK 13Wedding Business Strategy & Meta Ads+

Included free: connect creative skills with business planning.

  • Wedding package pricing, client contracts and campaign planning.
  • Facebook/Instagram ads, audience selection, budgets and ad creative design.
WEEK 14AI Automation, WhatsApp CRM & Client Communication+

Included free: organise enquiries and prepare for your next step.

  • WhatsApp lead follow-ups, portfolio website setup and sales conversations.
  • Course completion, certification and joining the alumni network.
