Module Detailed Syllabus Topics Industry Practical Application
Module 1: Workflow & Assembly Footage ingestion, project bin organization, sequence settings, three-point editing, J-cut, L-cut. Badi 500GB+ wedding raw projects ko clean arrange karke fast editing workflow setup karna.

Module 5: Exporting & Delivery Bitrates, 4K vs 1080p, Instagram Reels vertical 9:16 export, client review links, master archiving. Zero quality loss ke sath social media aur commercial clients ko video delivery.
