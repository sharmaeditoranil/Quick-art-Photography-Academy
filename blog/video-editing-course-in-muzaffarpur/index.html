Wedding Video Editing Classes in Muzaffarpur

Wedding photography और filmmaking में video editing का special role है। एक memorable wedding film बनाने के लिए music selection, cinematic transitions, storytelling और color correction पर खास ध्यान देना पड़ता है।

Wedding Video Editing Classes in Muzaffarpur में students दोनों तरह का काम सीखते हैं — EDIUS में multi-camera traditional full wedding film और Premiere Pro में cinematic highlights व teasers। Muzaffarpur और North Bihar के wedding season में ऐसे editors की हमेशा demand रहती है।

6-Week Course Syllabus

Week 01

Traditional Wedding Editing (EDIUS)

Multi-track workflow, 2–3 camera sync, titles, music placement और fast delivery exports।

Week 02

Cinematic Editing (Premiere Pro)

Project organisation, proxies, J/L-cuts, speed ramps, whip zooms और invisible cuts।

Week 03

Storytelling & Motion Graphics

Pacing, sound effects, ambient audio layering, motion titles और lower thirds।

Week 04

Music Videos, Teasers & Reels

Beat-sync editing, wedding teasers, vertical 9:16 reels और 4K export settings।

Week 05

DaVinci Resolve Color Grading

Nodes, lift/gamma/gain, curves, qualifiers, power windows और skin-tone correction।

Week 06

VFX, Audio & Final Wedding Film

Fusion titles, object removal, Fairlight audio mastering और final portfolio showreel।
