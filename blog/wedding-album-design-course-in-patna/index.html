Complete 6-Module Syllabus Breakdown: Wedding Album Design Course in Patna

हमारे Wedding Album Design Course in Patna का सिलेबस जीरो से शुरू होकर प्रिंट-रेडी एल्बम मास्टरपीस बनाने तक के सभी स्टेप्स को कवर करता है:

Photoshop Fundamentals & Workspace Calibration

Module 2

Photo Selection, Story Pacing & Sequence Planning

High-End Bridal Retouching & Color Correction

Camera Raw Filter, Exposure balancing, Frequency Separation skin retouching, Eye/teeth whitening, Dodge & Burn, Color harmony.

Creative Backgrounds & Masking Techniques

Pen tool, Object selection, Layer masks, Feather blending, Custom gradient backgrounds, Light bokeh, Vignettes and Texture overlays.

Module 5

Typography, Wedding Monograms & Cover Page Design
