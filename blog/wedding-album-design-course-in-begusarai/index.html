Photo Album Design Course in Begusarai: Complete Curriculum

Quick Art Photography Academy ka Photo Album Design Course in Begusarai beginners aur aspiring album designers ke liye practical learning par focus karta hai. Course me students ko Adobe Photoshop software ke advanced tools, photo editing, creative layouts, background designing aur professional album designing workflow ke bare me sikhaya jata hai.

1. Photoshop Tools, Selection & High-End Retouching

Pen Tool, Lasso Tool, Layer Masks, Frequency Separation skin retouching, blemish removal, glowing bridal skin tone create karna aur hair cutout techniques.

2. Creative Layouts, Spreads & Grid Systems

Standard 12x36, 12x30 aur 10x24 photobook spreads par grids aur guides setup karna, safe margins aur spine fold (crease) area ka dhyan rakhna jisse photos fold me na dabein.

3. Background Designing & Aesthetic Color Palettes

Cluttered ready-made templates ke badle clean custom textures, subtle gradients, floral elements aur couple dress ke colors ke sath matching backgrounds banana.

4. Color Grading, Lighting Balance & Lab Printing Standards

Adobe RGB vs sRGB color profiles, 300 DPI high-resolution output, CMYK lab calibration aur velvet, non-tearable, metallic sheets ke liye exact print export settings.
