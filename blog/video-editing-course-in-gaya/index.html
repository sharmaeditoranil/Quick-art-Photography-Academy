3. Software Taught & Module-Wise 6-Week Syllabus

Hamara curriculum market requirements aur latest 2026 industry standards ke mutabiq tayar kiya gaya hai:

🖥️ Softwares Covered in Hands-on Studio:

Adobe Premiere Pro 2026 DaVinci Resolve Studio (Color & Fairlight) EDIUS Pro (Fast Wedding Turnaround) Adobe Photoshop (Thumbnails & Layouts) Adobe After Effects (Motion Graphics) AI Video Tools (Auto Captions & Audio Cleanup)

📅 Week-by-Week Practical Roadmap:

  • Week 1: Traditional Wedding Video Editing with EDIUS Pro – Multi-camera timeline setup, 2 to 3 camera sync, fast song project cuts, title animation aur traditional wedding deliveries.
  • Week 2: Cinematic Storytelling with Adobe Premiere Pro – 4K ProRes proxy creation, chronological bins, J-cuts & L-cuts, speed ramping, invisible transitions aur audio sync.
  • Week 3: Motion Graphics, Lower Thirds & Vertical Content – Animated titles, Instagram Reels (9:16) editing, YouTube retention cuts, beat-matching aur dynamic sound layering.
  • Week 4: Professional Color Grading with DaVinci Resolve – Lift/Gamma/Gain, curves, power windows, Indian wedding skin tone evaluation (Haldi yellow-cast balance), signature LUT creation.
  • Week 5: Audio Design & Fairlight Mastering – Background noise removal, dialog leveling, ambient layering, wedding vow mastering aur cinematic sound effects.
  • Week 6: Final Wedding Film Project & Portfolio Showreel – Live wedding teaser & highlight submission, mentor review, 4K export presets, client delivery protocol aur freelancing guidance.
