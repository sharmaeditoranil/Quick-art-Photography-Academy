Course Modules You Will Learn at Quick Art Academy

Our comprehensive Album Design Course in Chapra curriculum is designed from beginner fundamentals all the way to master-level digital artistry:

Module 01

Introduction to Album Design & Layout Fundamentals

Understanding album sizes (12x36, 12x30, 10x24, 14x40 inches), aspect ratios, bleed lines, safe zones, margins, spine alignment, and core design principles for Indian wedding rituals (Haldi, Mehendi, Sangeet, Jaimala, Vidai).

Module 02

Adobe Photoshop CC & Lightroom Techniques

Non-destructive editing, camera RAW processing, batch color correction, skin smoothing, high-frequency separation, blemish removal, background cleanup, masking, blend modes, and AI Neural Filters for flawless bride and groom portraits.

Module 03

Creative Layout Design & Visual Storytelling

Creating dynamic spreads, multi-photo grids, full-bleed hero spreads, negative space utilization, color harmony between opposing pages, and structuring the entire wedding chronology into an emotionally compelling storybook.

Module 04

Typography, Text Styling & Graphical Embellishments

Selecting modern Hindi, English, and calligraphy fonts, designing couple monograms, royal crests, quotes, date badges, borders, flourishes, and subtle textures without cluttering the photographs.

Module 05

Cover Design & Print-Ready Export (Canvera & Karizma)

Designing acrylic covers, leatherette pads, wooden boxes, metallic embossing, and velvet sleeves. Configuring CMYK color profiles, 300 DPI resolutions, and export pipelines compatible with major photo printing labs across India.

Module 06

Freelancing, Portfolio Development & Client Handling

Creating an attractive digital portfolio, pricing your design services per sheet, presenting proofs online for client revisions, handling difficult feedback professionally, and marketing your services to photography studios.

✔ Outcome: By the end of this course, you will be fully confident in creating world-class, print-ready wedding albums, baby shoot photobooks, and model portfolios from scratch in record time!
