YOUR LEARNING ROADMAP

Master Class Wedding Filmmaking Course Details

12 weeks of hands-on shooting, photo retouching, video editing & DaVinci color grading + 2 free bonus weeks of business scaling & AI automation.

Download Full Syllabus (PDF)
100% Free Room Stay for Outside Students (Food Extra)
Part 1

Wedding Cinematography & Photo / Album Design

Mastering cameras, lighting setups, advanced beauty retouching, AI automation, and luxury album layouts.

WEEKS 01–06
Week 01 PRODUCTION

Professional Wedding Shooting & Camera Mastery

Camera handling (Sony/Canon), lens selections, shutter angles, picture profiles (Log/Cine), 3-point lighting setup, gimbal balancing, live bride & groom posing, and real wedding shoot simulation.

Week 02 LIGHTROOM CLASSIC

Wedding Photo Editing Workflow

RAW image sorting & culling (Photo Mechanic / Lightroom), bulk rating, color temperature correction, basic tone curve manipulation, HSL adjustments, and batch preset creation for weddings.

Week 03 PHOTOSHOP CC

Advanced Wedding Photo Retouching

Frequency separation, dodge & burn technique, skin texture preservation, blemish removal, background cleanup/replacement, jewelry pop-up, eye & teeth whitening, and bridal beauty retouching.

Week 04 AI WORKFLOW

AI & High-Speed Bulk Wedding Photo Editing

Using AI portrait tools, automatic subject selection, neural filters, high-volume batch retouching, automated sky replacement, and building an ultra-fast commercial delivery workflow.

Week 05 CREATIVE LAYOUT

Wedding Album Design & Storyboarding

Album dimensions & sizing rules, chronological storyboarding, background selection, color palette harmony, grid systems, typography, and premium wedding book design principles.

Week 06 CANVA & PRINT READY

Advanced Album Finishes & Printing

Designing with Photoshop & Canva, embossing, velvet/matte/glossy sheet selection, thermal lamination, bleed & crop marks, lab color calibration (sRGB vs CMYK), and final print export.

Download Full Syllabus (PDF)
Part 2

Video Editing, Sound Design & DaVinci Color Grading

From fast turnaround traditional wedding cuts to cinematic multicam edits, sound design, and Hollywood-grade color grading.

WEEKS 07–12
Week 07 EDIUS PRO

Traditional Wedding Video Editing with EDIUS Pro

Timeline setup, fast rough cut workflow, multi-camera syncing (audio sync), wedding song beat cutting, 3D transitions, regional wedding rituals highlight editing, and speedy client delivery.

Week 08 PREMIERE PRO 2026

Cinematic Video Editing Basics (Premiere Pro 2026)

Project organization, proxy workflow for 4K/6K footage, Lumetri scopes, speed ramping, J & L cuts, cinematic pacing, transition effects, and keyboard shortcuts for rapid cutting.

Week 09 STORYTELLING

Advanced Cinematic Storytelling & Motion Graphics

Crafting emotional wedding storylines, audio voice-over mixing, sound effect layering (whooshes, risers, ambience), dialogue enhancement, and animated title design with Essential Graphics.

Week 10 SOCIAL & TEASERS

Music Videos, Teasers, YouTube 4K & Viral Reels

Editing 60-second Instagram cinematic reels, vertical (9:16) framing, trending hooks, 3-minute cinematic teasers, complete wedding film master export settings for YouTube 4K.

Week 11 DAVINCI RESOLVE

DaVinci Resolve: Hollywood Color Grading

Node tree workflow, Log to Rec.709 conversion, color wheels & curves, matching shots from different cameras, skin-tone preservation with qualifiers, and applying cinematic wedding LUTs.

Week 12 VFX & MASTERING

DaVinci Fusion VFX, Audio Mastering & Final Wedding Film

Fusion title animations, sky replacement, object removal in video, Fairlight audio sweetening, noise reduction, loudness standards (-14 LUFS), and full project grading defense.

Part 3

Free Wedding Business, Marketing & AI Automation

Complimentary 2 bonus weeks to help you launch a high-earning wedding studio, run Meta ads, and close high-ticket clients.

WEEKS 13–14 (100% FREE BONUS)
Week 13 FREE BONUS

Wedding Photography Business Strategy & Meta Ads

Pricing psychology, high-ticket wedding package creation, portfolio presentation, Instagram & Facebook Meta ads targeting affluent brides/grooms, and lead generation funnels.

Week 14 CERTIFICATION

AI Automation, WhatsApp CRM, Client Closing & Certification

Automated WhatsApp follow-up bots, AI proposal generators, contract templates, overcoming client price objections, sales closing mastery, final practical exam, and certificate awarding ceremony.
