Anil Sharma · Founder & Lead Instructor

Anil Sharma started in the editing suite over a decade ago. Having directed and edited hundreds of cinematic wedding films, commercial adverts, and creative video campaigns, he noticed a major disconnect in conventional training institutes:

"Most editing courses only teach software shortcuts. They tell you which button to press, but they never teach you why to make a cut, how to hold an emotional frame, or how to negotiate with high-paying wedding clients. At Quick Art, students step into a real production studio from Day One."

Under his personal 1-on-1 mentorship, students assemble broadcast-grade wedding teaser sequences, master DaVinci Resolve color wheels, sculpt rich audio landscapes, and build the creative confidence required to establish profitable studios or work as senior post-production artists.

Our Vision
